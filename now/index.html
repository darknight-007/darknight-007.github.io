Now

October 2026

A dated page of what I am actually working on, in the spirit of /now. Updated when the season changes. Older entries stay below — the record keeps its history.

Working on

  • Engineering Mythology II. The 2026 incarnation of Navagunjara Reborn as a paper: the interregnum as dataset, the digital twin as the canonical persistent pattern, and reincarnation — regeneration under transformation — as a design pattern for ephemeral public art.
  • Spectral kernel dynamics for planetary surfaces. Bringing the kernel-dynamics framework to the lab's own ground: terrain as graphs, distinction dynamics, and what is conserved.
  • Calibrating twins against the world. The lab's central open problem: whether inference on a twin transfers to the original. The balanced-rock experiments say how far a simulation can be trusted.
  • This site. Bringing the public record current — the indices had frozen at different years, and none of them contained the present program.
